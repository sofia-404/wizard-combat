# The brandbook makes one original UI system easy to reuse.

Target: `dist/brandbook.html` and reusable files under `dist/brand/`.

Mode: Read. Embedded screen demonstrations mix Experience and Operate. The audience is the adult player's product team: the reader needs to understand the identity, inspect states, and apply the same rules to future UI.

The user delegated completion. The audience was confirmed as adults. Palette direction is supplied; materials, typography, layout, and motion are design decisions. This work uses authored vector diagrams and code, with no generated environment artwork and no stored global build-path preference.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Make a deliberate hand movement the identity. Teach the sequence through the visual system and show it in three honest screen studies.

OWN-WORLD: Midnight navy and teal fields, restrained aged-gold structure, cyan active traces, open measured arcs, anchored strokes, terminal diamonds, engraved serif titles, readable humanist sans-serif controls.

STORY: Understand the mark and palette, switch among menu/training/fight studies, simulate a cast, inspect reusable controls, then use the repository specifications.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A large two-line title and brief explanation sit left. An original anchored gesture study occupies the right. A clear link leads directly to screen previews. The upper navigation gives access to the complete reference.

FORM: Arc & Anchor, a movement-notation system expressed as a readable field reference. The direction-seed call completed with key `e360d622` and assigned index 6. The delegated design preserves the supplied palette and adult audience. No claim is made that the user selected a generated concept card.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Challenger assessment

Assessments use adult audience identification and product clarity. They are design judgments, not user selections.

| Source system | Verdict | Reason and retained discipline |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brick assembly instructions | Competitive on clarity | Ordered movement teaching is useful; toy materials do not suit the adult direction. Keep explicit start, direction, end. |
| Daylight architectural section | Declined | Solar paths imply unrelated controls and dilute casting. Keep the discipline of protecting useful light and readable dark zones. |
| Airport wayfinding | Competitive on clarity | Immediate decisions help, but the transport grammar loses the fantasy setting. Keep one obvious next action and consistent labels. |
| Parametric identity | Competitive on mechanism | Gesture-derived shape is relevant; reseeding palette and layout would obscure stable feedback. Keep the fixed original mark and input-linked trace. |
| Cracktro depth queue | Declined | Bitmap type and depth-ranked tasks weaken reading and fantasy identification. Keep still foreground text and receding background activity. |
| Film cutting bench | Declined | Perforated rails and edit bins imply unrelated work. Keep state markers that remain legible without color. |

## Practical defaults and limits

All game UI, brandbook copy, accessible labels, examples, and repository design documentation use English. The user confirmed this language requirement. Keep existing spell terminology until a separate naming decision. Bundled fonts retain their full character coverage.

Main-menu and fight studies are proposed designs. The training study simulates feedback and does not open a camera. The current game continues at the root route. Do not treat illustrated controls as new gameplay behavior.
