Method: segmentation and inpainting of dist/assets/practice-arena-background.jpg. No image generation.
Script: scripts/generate-practice-arena-idle-art.py. The arena image is unchanged.
Assets: dist/assets/practice-arena-player-plate.png, practice-arena-player-body.png, practice-arena-player-wand-arm.png, and practice-arena-player-back-arm.png.
Canvas: arena pixels 0,240 to 660,810.

Matte: BiRefNet general model with a Swin-T backbone (MIT license), in the ONNX export from the rembg project.
Model: https://github.com/danielgatis/rembg/releases/download/v0.0.0/BiRefNet-general-bb_swin_v1_tiny-epoch_232.onnx
SHA-256: 5600024376f572a557870a5eb0afb1e5961636bef4e1e22132025467d0f03333
Run: CPU, 1024x1024 input. Tools: ONNX Runtime 1.30.0, OpenCV 4.13.0, NumPy 2.4.6, Pillow 12.3.0.

Fill: OpenCV Telea inpainting starts 2 pixels outside the matte. Only thin edge strips of the fill come into view during the motion.
Edges: each layer pixel over the fill gives the arena pixel again. The arm layers fade in at the shoulders over a hidden copy in the body layer.
Check: at rest, the layers reproduce the arena pixels within one color level.
The dummy uses the existing practice-arena-dummy-unarmed.png and practice-arena-wand.png. It needs no new artwork.
